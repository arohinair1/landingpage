How to play

Estimate the angle between the two rays, following the burgundy arc clockwise. The marked angle can be anything from 1° to 359°, including angles larger than a half-turn.

You get one guess per round. The answer appears immediately, with your estimate drawn as a dashed ray. Earn up to 100 points: 100 for an exact guess, losing points linearly with absolute error, down to 0 at 180° off. Your score runs for this page session. Play as many rounds as you like.

Inspired by Angledle. Independently written, with original styling and an immediate-reveal format rather than its six-guess daily puzzle.

COMPUTER SCIENCE & ECONOMICS SENIOR THESIS

Making medical image segmentation more adaptable

Yale Vision Laboratory

Neighboring slices · Conceptual illustration

This thesis explores sequential test-time adaptation for medical image segmentation, asking whether predictions from neighboring image slices can help models adapt to unfamiliar data.

The approach combines image registration to align predictions between slices with energy-based adaptation to encourage anatomically plausible segmentations. The work examines when adaptation helps, when it doesn’t, and what explains those differences.
